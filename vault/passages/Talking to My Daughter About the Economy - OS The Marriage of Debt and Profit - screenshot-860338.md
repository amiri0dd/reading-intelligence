Do you see now how debt, not coal, was the real
fuel that powered the engine of the Industrial
Revolution, generating mountains of wealth for a
few and unspeakable misery for the rest? In market
societies all wealth is nourished by debt and all of
the unimaginable riches created over the past three
centuries ultimately owe their existence to debt.

as Doctor Faustus shows us, is to market societies what
hell is to Christianity: unpleasant yet indispensable.
